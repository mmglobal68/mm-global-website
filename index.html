ABOUT US

Our Story

MM Global began where every Pakistani seller begins: doing it ourselves. Mohsin has been in e-commerce since 2019 — selling on Amazon, eBay and Walmart, and importing products at wholesale level. Mubashar has been in social media marketing since 2018 and running Shopify stores and brands since 2020 — building stores, running ads, launching products and dealing with real customers every single day.

For years, people around us kept asking how online selling actually works — and we saw the same problem everywhere. They didn't need more theory. They needed someone who had actually done it, showing them step by step.

So the two partners joined forces to build MM Global: one place where you can learn eBay, Walmart, Amazon and Shopify the practical way, import products the right way, or hand your accounts and brand to a team that runs them for you. Our promise is simple: we teach what we practice — and we practice what we teach.
